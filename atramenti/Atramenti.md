"My reign was accented by the War of the Bookkeepers. A conflict that tore at the threads of the Atramenti itself. I know in time you will find our lands with freshly sewn power balances amongst the Dukes, emboldened raids from the south, talks of unification to our west, an ever narrowing forest line to our east, and the centre of our faith bring a new concern of discordance. I pray to Amar he will grant me time to steward you into a fine enough Emperor to weather these storms."
- A royal letter from Emperor Maxil IV three weeks before his passing.

 - A letter bearing the mark of Hoff addressed to Suudpost.

- A declaration from the Duke of Denbur.
