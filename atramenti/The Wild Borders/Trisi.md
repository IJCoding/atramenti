The nation of Trisi is a decentralised federation of tribes, towns and cities reeling from the dissolution of the Fallen Empire. Its people, unlike the Cineri, seek to return to the "old ways" of governance. This has reopened a conflict that predated the Fallen Empire. The border is heavily defended by both if not more visibly done so by those of the Empire. A curious note is that many border town people of the Empire travel out to Trisi lands, seemingly willingly, and rarely return.
