Foria is a land of mist and wood. The beings there, the Forians, traverse the densely packed lands with ease. It is said that weapons and tools are one and the same - both serve a purpose after all. The Old Empire's traversal into Forian lands was met with the silence this land is famed for. Marking initial success for the fledgling colony. However, when that old Empire awaited an update on the colony's status, silence is all they received.
