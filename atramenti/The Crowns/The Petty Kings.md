In a state where the lines between civil war and war have blurred entirely the area known as The Petty Kings is a land of constantly shifting, alliances, and borders. The cultures here are said to be unlike most thought they all worship saints the differences can be stark. These differences have sparked constant conflict and forged many alliances. The one known time of peace was that under the Fallen Empire. With many divisions it is hard to say if the land will ever see peace again - unless its subjugated once more.
