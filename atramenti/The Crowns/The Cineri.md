The Cineri Empire, also known as the Remnant Empire, is a collection of duchies, counties, baronies, and Free Cities.

Baronies serve counties, counties serve duchies, duchies and Free Cities serve the Emperor.

Whilst war inside and outside the empire does occur and is often legal, declaration against a Free City is against the Emperor - as they exist directly under his protection.

Though inheritance in much of the empire is hereditary, the title of Emperor itself is elective. Certain duchies within are granted Elector status. Upon the death of the Emperor the electors convene and a new emperor is chosen. For several generations now the line has come from the same family. When Maxil IV died his son taking the role was unexpected only due to his age, not due to his lineage. A nine year old emperor can be dangerous, though a regency council is appointed the emperor - even in youth - makes the final decisions.
