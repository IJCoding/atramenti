The "faith" of the Trisi is not fully understood in the Empire due to those who practice being solitary by nature. It's was, for the most part eradicated by The Old Empire prior to it's collapse. The faith's focus on indiviudalism being antithetical to the way of life the concurrent Emperors sought to establish. Though no established church there are central beliefs held across denominations and sects. Many belief it to be an offshoot of a precursor to Imperian as many of the titans exist across both. As empirians see the conuctio as a joyous occasion the Trisi see it as the end of times when all is one.
