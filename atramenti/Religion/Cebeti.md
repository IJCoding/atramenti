Also called 'The Restraints of Cebeti' it acts as the dominant faith of the Lands of [Ulodana](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/arkthan-misterjaye/a/ulodana-article) and the [Topologian March](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/arkthan-misterjaye/a/topologian-march-article). The focus being on how restraint is not a potentiality of power but required component, without constraint power is a daemonic force of evil. 
*Do not let your left hand touch that which your right cannot grasp. To do so is to invoke daemonic attraction within oneself.* - Nizar Yato

 *Worn iron must never touch blood of another and may never be worn in comfort till it touches your own. - Ihab Yato*

 *Drink not from the well that quenches no mouths. For the salt within is a manifestation of brimestone. - Munsif Yato*

 *He whos right hand touched last the flesh of fruit or beast may never eat of it until proper structure established - Nizar Yato*

*To make fire is not to shame the sun but to accept its greatness cannot be matched - Lutfiyya Oja*

 *Strike the slave not for rebellion but so rebellion is only done with purpose - Balj Asult*

 Only through dutiful acts befitting of ones station may ascension through the structure manifest. Greater power is prefaced with greater burdens and in many cases physical chains. With the final step being that of god-hood ascension. Hierarchies are challenged often and rebellion in of itself is not disloyalty but an opportunity for one to re-establish proper order and structure.
