The collection of gods and creators primarily worshipped by in the Southern Frosts.
 
"From the nothing, Ystri stepped. She shot shards of light in all directions and saw the nothingness. Grabbing a shard of light she stabbed herself igniting her skin and causing blood to flow. The flame bore Aestra lord of the flame. The falling blood cooled forming Cera. As Cera froze her surroundings and Aestra spread his flame earth was formed. Aestra's embers formed the wind and melted Cera's ice forming water. From the water and earth grew trees and from the trees stepped Korun and Rakiri the first of the gods.
  
Korun tore at the earth and Rakiri stared at the three. Rakiri howled at the wind forming speech. Rakiri now hearing spoke the words of the three. Rakiri spoke with Korun and taught him the ways of Aestra. He burned and crushed the ground forming clean metal. The pair tried to speak the words of Ystri but it tore through their throats tearing flesh and blood. Their blood fell on the earth and from it grew Dinavi. Blood that fell on the metal formed Dwarves. Blood that fell on the plants grew Elves, they fled from the creators.
  
Hushed in tone Rakiri and Korun whispered to their children. The Dwarves obeyed dutifully the words of Korun. The Dinavi closed their eyes and listened but the words of Rakiri and Korun could not be heard. Some of the Dinavi ventured far to find the creators.
  
Aided by Dwarves some found Aestra his voice sprung forth through mines burning many. Those who dug through fire but did not burn and saw Aestra could hear the words of Rakiri and Korun.
  
Some ventured to the southern reaches finding Cera in her isolation the biting cold of her whispers froze many in fear - those who walked through frost and saw Cera could hear the words of Rakiri and Korun.
  
Others stole wood from the Elves, hiding with others who fled from the creators. These Dinavi fashioned ships and ventured to the oceans. Shards fell and broke many vessels. - those who saw Ystri and returned could hear the words of Rakiri and Korun.
  
Those who spoke the words of the three and heard the words of the two were shielded. Runes marked their flesh and homes.
  
The three balance but are never equal. A winter will come where Ystri will rule thrice. Cera will not be weak as frost descends into the lands of Aestra. Aestra will not be without strength as land will be scorched and forests engulfed in his presence. Yet Ystri will rise above the others through volition or star-guide. Kings will rule nought but have their greatest armies. Sages will have runes abundant yet speak no truth and the peasants will harvest but bowls are left unfilled. The end time is the second test of the world and will let us speak the words of the three, even that which hurts the two."
- The words of runemaster Thorkell
